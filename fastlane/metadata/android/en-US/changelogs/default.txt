What's new in 1.6.1

• App manager: tapping an app's APK shows its contents again
• Ticking boxes quickly down a column no longer opens the rows in between
• A fast double tap no longer opens an item twice
• Pictures and videos sit on a checkerboard, so dark and transparent ones keep a visible edge
• Shizuku-compatible services such as Shizako are recognised
• Root: when su is refused, XFiles says where to allow it (Magisk 31.1 and later no longer ask)
