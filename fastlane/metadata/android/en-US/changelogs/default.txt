What's new in 1.5.0

• Recycle Bin: deletes on internal storage, SD cards and USB drives can be restored, deleted permanently or emptied from the new Recycle Bin root
• Use XFiles to view PDFs and text files opened from other apps (turn on in Settings)
• Text editor opens large files: loads only what you edit, with undo/redo, Wrap lines and GBK detection
• Folder rows show item counts and dates
• Predictive back, and smoother screen switches on older phones
