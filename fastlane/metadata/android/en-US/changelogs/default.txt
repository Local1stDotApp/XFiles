What's new in 1.6.0

• New built-in archive reader: 7Z and TAR.XZ open several times faster, and ISO, CPIO, DEB, ZST, LZ4 and more now open too
• Search the whole tree or all storage, then jump to a result
• PDF viewer: select and copy text
• Media player: seek, replay, change speed and keep watching in picture-in-picture
• Text viewer: right-to-left text and legacy encodings
• APK and split bundle files show their app icons
• Dialogs keep their buttons on screen with long names
